Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Grown with Passion

Malik Dhakki Dates Nursery Farms was born from a deep love for the land. For over many years (This Business is passed down through generations. ), our family has nurtured date palms under the open sky, using traditional methods combined with modern agricultural knowledge. Every plant we grow and every date we harvest carries the care and dedication of generations.

We supply premium Kanjor plants, fresh Kanjor, and sun-dried Chawara to farmers, wholesalers, exporters, and households across the country and beyond.
